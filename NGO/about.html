About Helping Hands

Connecting people who need help with people who want to help.

Who We Are

Helping Hands is a community-driven NGO platform where anyone can request help or support someone in need. Our mission is to ensure that no person feels alone during difficult times.


Anyone can post verified requests for food, clothes, education, medicines, shelter, blood donation, emergency support, or financial assistance. Volunteers and donors can directly connect and help.

Our Mission

To build India's most trusted community helping platform.

Our Vision

No one should suffer because they cannot ask for help.

Our Values

Trust, Transparency, Humanity and Respect.
